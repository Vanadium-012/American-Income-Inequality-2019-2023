All the data associated with this analysis of the American state of Arkansas comes from the state's Department of Shared Administrative Services' Arkansas GIS Office and the National Institute on Minority Health and Health Disparities.
The data regarding the information on Arkansas' food insecurity is based on the NIH's "Income (Median household income) for Arkansas by County All Races (includes Hispanic/Latino), Both Sexes, All Ages, 2019-2023
Sorted by Value (Dollars)"
Each piece of information is accounted for each county and is reflected in the attached metadata and completed uploaded map.
