# Analyzing Food Insecurity in the State of Arkansas (2019 - 2023)
In an attempt to understand the myriad of factors that have lead to the state of Arkansas being one of the highest states with the highest food insecurity rates, as noted by the [USDA](https://www.ers.usda.gov/topics/food-nutrition-assistance/food-security-in-the-us/key-statistics-graphics) in 2024 with this report. The article was created by *Matthew P. Rabbitt, Madeline Reed-Jones, Laura J. Hales*, can be seen [here](https://www.ers.usda.gov/publications/113622).

Located in their own folder are some key visualizations created during the analysis process. Each of them are described below:
#### Income Map for Arkansas (2019 - 2023)
![Arkansas income map](https://github.com/Vanadium-012/American-Income-Inequality-2019-2023/blob/Arkansas/Arkansas-income-map.png)

A simple overview of the financial situation in Arkansas, with the financial data coming from the *National Institute on Minority Health and Health Disparities.* Here it's easy to see how Mississippi Delta counties are experiencing lower wages compared to northwestern or central counties.

#### Obesity Rates in 2022
![Obesity Rates among Children and Adults](https://github.com/Vanadium-012/American-Income-Inequality-2019-2023/blob/Arkansas/Arkansas%20Food%20Insecurity%20Visualizations/Obesity%20Rates%2C%202022.png)

This bar chart visualization depicts obesity rates across children and adult subsets of the population, with additional data on which ethnicities are most adversely affected by obesity. The data shows that Hispanic children lead in this category at 0.53%, with Black children second at 0.49%. As for childhood obesity rates per county, Monroe County leads at 0.56%, and is tied with Chicot, with Bradley (0.54%), and Lee Counties (0.53%).

Among adults, Ouachita County leads in the data at 0.80%, followed by Pike (0.79%), with Nevada County in third (0.79% also). As for obesity among adults, leading in this data are multiracial residents (non-Hispanic) at 76%, with Hispanics second at (73%), and Black Arkansans tied with Indigenous Americans at (72%).
